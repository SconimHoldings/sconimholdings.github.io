Website privacy

This page covers this website. For the app, see the Embody Privacy Policy.

This website doesn’t use cookies, analytics, advertising or any other trackers. It is hosted on Netlify, which may keep visitors’ IP addresses in its logs to keep the service secure.

If you send us a message with the contact form, your name, email address and message go to Netlify, which checks them for spam (using Akismet), keeps them for us and forwards them to our inbox. We use them only to reply to you, and you can ask us to delete them at any time. If you email us instead, we use your message only to reply to you.
